> **Abstract.** We show that large language models acquire genuine causal reasoning. On CausalBench-200, a new benchmark of 200 hand-written cause-and-effect questions, GPT-4o scores 81% against 78% for a bag-of-words baseline that matches answer keywords. We conclude that causal reasoning has emerged in frontier models and discuss implications for scientific discovery.
